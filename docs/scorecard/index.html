AI Sales Readiness Scorecard

Five questions. See where your sales team stands on putting AI to work, and get a straight read on the gaps costing you revenue right now. Nothing to install, no email required to see your score.

1. Does your sales team run on a documented, repeatable process?

2. Can you forecast the quarter within 10 percent?

3. Is anyone on the team already using AI in their daily selling?

4. Do reps get consistent coaching on real calls?

5. Have you defined what good AI use looks like for the team?
